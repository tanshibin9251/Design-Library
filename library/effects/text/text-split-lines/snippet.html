Scroll to read

Every line finds its own moment to arrive.

Lines are measured from the live layout, so the split follows the real wrapping on any screen and rebuilds when the window changes. Each line rises as it enters the viewport, and slips back down if you scroll the other way.

Because every line is driven by where it sits on screen, there are no timers and no staggers to tune. Long paragraphs reveal at the speed you read them.

The original text stays in the page for screen readers and search, and users who prefer reduced motion simply get the normal paragraph.

Nothing is hidden for good. Everything is just a little late.
