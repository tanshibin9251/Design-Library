The itinerary

Nine days, in order.

1

Day 1–2 · Arrive in Svolvær

Land, drop bags and walk to the harbour for a first look at the light. Briefing over dinner.

2

Day 3 · Reine at dusk

Red cabins, black water and the first aurora forecast. Our first real blue hour.

3

Day 4–5 · The beaches

Haukland and Uttakleiv at low tide, then a long evening on the dunes with the sea at our back.

4

Day 6 · Weather day

Rest, edit and print. If the sky opens, we go; if not, nobody is sorry.

5

Day 7–8 · Henningsvær

Football pitch on a rock, harbour reflections and the last aurora window of the trip.

6

Day 9 · Farewell

A last sunrise at the fjord, a final edit over breakfast and a flight home with a full card.

Good to know
What are the odds of seeing aurora?

In a nine-day window, high. We have seen it on 8 of the last 9 trips, but we never promise a sky.

How cold does it get?

Around −5 to −10 °C at night, with wind. We send a layering guide and lend spikes.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Yes. We run a short exposure workshop on day one.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund up to 90 days before departure, 50% up to 45 days. Insurance is required.
