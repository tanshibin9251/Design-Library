Why did it sink?

Eight things that go wrong, and what to change.

My cake sank in the middle

The oven door opened too early, or the oven ran cold. Wait until the last 5 minutes before checking, and use an oven thermometer.

Cookies spread into one sheet

Butter too warm or too little flour. Chill the dough for 30 minutes and weigh the flour.

Tart base shrank

Rest the dough in the tin for 30 minutes before blind-baking, and don't stretch it.

Dense, heavy crumb

Over-mixing after adding flour, or old baking powder. Fold gently; test the powder in hot water.
