Vanilla sponge cake

EASY · 55 MIN

A light, even sponge that holds frosting and fruit. One bowl, five ingredients, no creaming marathon.

Ingredients · 20 cm round

  • Butter, soft150 g
  • Caster sugar150 g
  • Eggs3
  • Self-raising flour150 g
  • Milk2 tbsp
  • Vanilla extract2 tsp

Different pan?

  1. Heat the oven to 170°C fan. Grease and line a 20 cm round tin.
  2. Beat butter and sugar for 3 minutes until pale. Add eggs one at a time, with a spoon of flour each.
  3. Fold in the rest of the flour, milk and vanilla until just combined.
  4. Bake 28–32 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes.
