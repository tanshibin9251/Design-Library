FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Is there a minimum?

Private banking begins at RM 3 million in assets. Wealth planning has no minimum for existing clients.

How are you paid?

By a published, transparent fee. We receive no commissions from product providers.

Who holds my assets?

Assets are held in your name with independent custodians. Gilded Ledger never pools client money.

Can my advisers be involved?

Always. We work alongside your lawyers and accountants.

How do I begin?

Request a meeting. A partner will reply within one working day.

Is everything confidential?

Yes. Client names are never shared and we do not advertise.
