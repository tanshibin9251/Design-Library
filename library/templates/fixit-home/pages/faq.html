FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

How fast can a technician come?

Most bookings made before 2 pm are served the same day. Emergencies are attended within 60 minutes in the Klang Valley.

Do I pay a call-out fee?

A call-out is RM 60, which is credited to your bill if you go ahead with the repair.

Are your technicians licensed?

Yes. Plumbers and electricians hold the relevant local licences and every technician is insured.

What does the warranty cover?

Workmanship and parts we supply are covered for 90 days. If the same fault returns, we fix it free.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cards, bank transfer, Touch 'n Go and cash. You pay after the job is done.

Which areas do you cover?

Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Subang, Shah Alam, Puchong, Klang, Cheras and Ampang.
