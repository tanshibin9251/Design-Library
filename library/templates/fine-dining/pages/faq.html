FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Do you cater for allergies?

Yes. Please tell us when booking and our kitchen will adapt every course.

Is there a dress code?

Smart. Jackets are optional; shorts and sandals are not.

How far ahead should I book?

Weekends fill three weeks ahead; midweek is usually easier.

Do you serve children?

Guests of twelve and over are welcome at dinner.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free until 48 hours before. After that the deposit applies.

Is parking available?

Complimentary valet from 6 pm.
