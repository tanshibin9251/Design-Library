The pantry · 调料

Eight bottles that make most of the recipes here.

酱

Light soy sauce

For salt and savoury depth. Reach for this first.

醋

Chinkiang vinegar

Dark, malty, gently sour. Dips and braises.

酒

Shaoxing wine

Removes gaminess and adds fragrance.

豆

Doubanjiang

Fermented chilli bean paste. Fry it in oil first.

椒

Sichuan pepper

Numbing, citrusy. Toast and grind fresh.

油

Sesame oil

A finishing drizzle, never for frying.
