麻婆豆腐 · Mapo tofu

Silky tofu in a red, numbing sauce. The version you can make on a Tuesday, with one jar of doubanjiang.

10 minPrep
15 minCook
MediumHeat
SichuanRegion

Ingredients

2servings

    1. Blanch the tofu

      Cut into 2 cm cubes. Simmer in salted water for 2 minutes to firm it up, then drain gently.

    2. Fry the base

      Brown the mince, then add doubanjiang and black beans and fry until the oil turns red.

    3. Simmer

      Add stock and tofu. Slide the pan rather than stir, so the cubes stay whole. Four minutes.

    4. Thicken and finish

      Pour in the slurry in two goes. Finish with ground Sichuan pepper and spring onion.
