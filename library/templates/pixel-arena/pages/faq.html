FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Do I need to bring my own gear?

No. Every station has a gaming mouse, keyboard and headset. You are welcome to bring your own.

What games are installed?

Valorant, CS2, Dota 2, League, Apex, FC and 150 more. Ask us if you want something added.

Is there an age limit?

Under-16s are welcome until 8 pm with a guardian's consent. Late-night sessions are 16 and above.

How do tournaments work?

Sign up at the counter or online, pay the entry, and we seed the bracket on the day.

Can I rent the whole arena?

Yes, for corporate days, launches and school groups. Request a quote for pricing.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, from RM 30 at the counter.
