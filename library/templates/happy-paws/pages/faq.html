FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome for the shop. Please book for vet visits and grooming.

Do you see cats and rabbits?

Yes, and small pets such as hamsters and guinea pigs.

What about emergencies?

Call our 24/7 line and we will meet you at the clinic.

Are my pets stressed by grooming?

We use calm, low-stress handling and take breaks as needed.

Can I pay by instalment?

Yes, 0% instalments are available on treatments above RM 500.

Is parking available?

Yes, free parking right outside.
