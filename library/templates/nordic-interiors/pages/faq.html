FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

How many projects do you take on?

Eight a year, so every project has a senior designer throughout.

Do you work on small spaces?

Yes. Some of our favourite projects are under 800 sq ft.

What is your style?

Quiet, natural and edited, with warm woods, linen and plenty of light.

Can you work with a contractor I already have?

Of course. We can design only, or manage the build as well.

How long does a project take?

A typical home takes four to six months from first visit to handover.

Do you work outside KL?

Yes, across Malaysia and Singapore.
