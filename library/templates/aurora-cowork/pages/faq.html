FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Can I try before I commit?

Yes. Book a tour and we will give you a free day pass to try the space.

What is included in membership?

Fibre internet, coffee and tea, printing credits, meeting-room hours and access to member events.

Is the space open 24 hours?

Resident and Studio members have 24/7 access. Flex members use the space 8 am to 10 pm.

How do meeting rooms work?

Book from the member app. Rooms have screens, video conferencing and whiteboards.

Can I cancel my membership?

Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. Studios have a six-month minimum.

Do you offer virtual offices?

Yes, with a registered address, mail handling and 4 free meeting-room hours a month.
