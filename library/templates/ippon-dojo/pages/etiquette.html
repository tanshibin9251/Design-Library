礼法

Six small rules that make the room feel the way it does.

  1. Bow at the doorStop, bow toward the shomen, then enter.
  2. Be earlyTen minutes before class, changed and warmed up.
  3. Clean uniform, clean feetShort nails, no jewellery, no shoes on the mat.
  4. Listen firstWhen sensei speaks, kneel and listen.
  5. Partner's safetyControl every technique. Tap early, tap often.
  6. Leave it cleanEveryone wipes the mat at the end of class.
