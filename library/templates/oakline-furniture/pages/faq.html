FAQ

Can I change the size?

Yes. Width and length can be adjusted in 5 cm steps at no extra cost.

Do you deliver outside Klang Valley?

Peninsular Malaysia, with a delivery fee by distance.

What if it does not fit through the door?

Tell us the lift and stair dimensions at checkout. We can ship large pieces knocked down.

Can I return a made-to-order piece?

Custom-size pieces are final. Standard sizes: 14 days, 15% restocking fee.
