Why we stopped doing mood boards

The one-page brief that replaced three weeks of Pinterest.

JJun Kai Ong12 March 2026

Good writing starts with a clear question. In this article we explore the idea in plain language, with examples you can use straight away.

The first thing to understand is that small decisions compound. Choosing carefully at the start saves far more time than it costs, and it makes every later step easier.

We have tested these approaches with real clients over several years. Some worked immediately; others needed adjusting. Here is what we learned.

Three things to remember

  • Start with the outcome you want.
  • Test small before you commit.
  • Write down what you learn.
