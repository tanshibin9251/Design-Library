会席

Eight courses, one tray at a time. The menu follows the week, so this list is only a guide.

  1. 先付

    Sakizuke

    A single bite to open: sesame tofu with fresh wasabi.

  2. 椀物

    Wanmono

    Clear soup with river fish and yuzu peel.

  3. 造り

    Tsukuri

    Sashimi of the day, cut thick and eaten with local salt.

  4. 焼物

    Yakimono

    Grilled trout, charcoal, one bay leaf.

  5. 煮物

    Nimono

    Simmered vegetables from the hill garden.

  6. 御飯

    Gohan

    Terrace rice cooked in an iron pot, with pickles and miso soup.

  7. 水菓子

    Mizugashi

    Seasonal fruit and a small sweet; matcha or roasted tea.
