The water

Soft, slightly alkaline spring at 42°C, drawn from beneath the ridge. Good for the skin; best taken slowly, twice a day.

Before you enter
  1. 一

    Wash first

    Sit and wash completely before entering the bath.

  2. 二

    Towel stays out

    Keep the small towel on your head or on the rim, not in the water.

  3. 三

    Tattoos welcome

    Private baths are available and tattoos are fine in all of them.
