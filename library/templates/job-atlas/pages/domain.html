How the field works

Money comes in as deposits, goes out as loans; the gap pays everyone’s salary.

Upstream

Central bank rules, depositors who park savings, and technology vendors who keep the systems running.

The field

Retail, commercial and Islamic banks. Branches take deposits; credit teams decide who can borrow and how much.

Downstream

Households buying homes, small businesses needing working capital, and large companies raising funds.

How a bank makes money

Mostly the gap between the interest it pays on deposits and the interest it charges on loans, plus fees for cards, transfers and advice. That is why the people who judge risk well — credit analysts — sit close to the core of the business.

What’s changing

  • Branch counters are shrinking as everyday banking moves to apps; teller roles are evolving into advice roles.
  • Risk, compliance and data skills are in short supply, so analysts with Excel and basic SQL stand out.
  • Chartered Banker and credit certificates are the common route from analyst to manager.
