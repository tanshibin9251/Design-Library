How this atlas is made

Every profile follows the same eight-part shape so you can compare roles without re-learning the page. Each is written for a reader who knows nothing about the industry.

  1. Research. Job ads, industry bodies and salary surveys are read first; interviews fill the gaps.
  2. Draft. Plain words, no buzzwords, and real software names instead of “office tools”.
  3. Check. Where a number has no source, we say so rather than guess.
  4. Review & date. Every profile shows when it was last checked.

Reading the salary ladder

Each bar is the usual monthly range in ringgit for that level, not a promise. Junior is roughly 0–2 years, experienced 3–6, senior 8 and up. The figures in this template are illustrative; replace them with your own checked data.
