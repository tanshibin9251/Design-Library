About us

A quiet room, a warm towel, an unhurried hour.

Still Water opened in 2019 with three rooms and one belief: rest is a skill worth practising. Everything here — light, scent, sound — is chosen to help you slow down.

We are a team of twelve therapists, all trained in Bali, Bangkok and Kuala Lumpur.
