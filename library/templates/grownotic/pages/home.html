Before you ask

The questions I get most often.

Still unsure about something? Ask directly. An answer costs nothing.

[FAQ 草稿——答案是我按你的现有文案和流程推的，请你改成你真实的答案]

What do I need to prepare before we start?

Your business details, a sense of who your customers are, and any logos or photos you already have. I will tell you exactly what else is needed after the discovery call.

Do you do the design and the SEO, or just the build?

Both. The site is built with on-page SEO and analytics in place before launch, so you are not paying twice to fix it later.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes. It is a clean Elementor build, and I walk you through it at handover.

How long does a company site take?

Typically 3 to 4 weeks from start to launch, depending on how quickly content comes in.

How many rounds of changes are included?

Two rounds of revisions, so the project does not turn into an endless loop.

Do you only work with businesses in Johor Bahru?

I am based in Johor Bahru and work with businesses across Malaysia.
