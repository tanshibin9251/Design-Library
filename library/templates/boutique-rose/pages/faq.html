FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

How long does delivery take?

West Malaysia 2–3 working days, East Malaysia 4–6, Singapore 5–7.

Can I return a sale item?

Yes, within 14 days if unworn with tags. Skincare must be unopened.

Do you ship overseas?

We ship to Singapore, Brunei and Thailand, with more countries coming.

What sizes do you offer?

XS to XXL. See the size guide for measurements.

How do I look after silk?

Hand wash cold or dry clean. Every piece has a care card.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, digital and printed, from RM 50.
