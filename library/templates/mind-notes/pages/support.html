Getting support

Reading is not treatment. Here is where to go when you need more.

If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number now, or go to the nearest emergency department.

Talking to someone

A family doctor or general practitioner is a good first step. They can talk with you, check for physical causes and refer you to a counsellor, psychologist or psychiatrist.

What this site is

Mind Notes is educational. It cannot diagnose, treat or replace professional care.
