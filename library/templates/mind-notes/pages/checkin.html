A gentle check-in

Six statements about the last two weeks. About two minutes. Nothing is stored.

1. I've felt on edge or unable to relax.

2. I've had trouble switching my thoughts off at night.

3. I've felt I had too much to do and too little time.

4. I've snapped at people or felt irritable.

5. I've found it hard to enjoy things I normally like.

6. I've felt overwhelmed by small decisions.
