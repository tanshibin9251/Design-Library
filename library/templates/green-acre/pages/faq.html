FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Where do you deliver?

Klang Valley, Ipoh and Cameron Highlands, with more areas coming.

Is the farm certified organic?

Yes. We hold MyOrganic certification and are audited every year.

Can I choose what is in my box?

You can mark up to three items you do not want each week.

What if I am away?

Skip any week up to midnight on Sunday, free of charge.

How is it packed?

Plastic-free, in returnable crates and compostable liners.

Can I visit the farm?

Yes. Farm days run every Saturday and booking is simple.
