How the council works

Who decides what, who pays for what, and how to have your say.

Council, committees and staff

Twenty-four elected councillors set policy at full-council meetings. Four committees look at planning, finance, community and the environment. Around 600 staff deliver the services, from rubbish collection to building approvals.

Every vote is published with the paperwork seven days beforehand, and anyone may speak for three minutes in public question time.
