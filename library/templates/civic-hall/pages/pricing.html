Pricing questions

How do I pay my assessment tax?

Online through the resident portal, at the counter, or at any participating bank branch. You will need your account number from the bill.

How long does a licence take?

Straightforward applications are approved in five working days. Premises requiring inspection take up to three weeks.

Can I attend council meetings?

Yes. All full council meetings are open to the public. Register at the front desk or watch the live stream.

Who do I call for a blocked drain?

Use the Report an issue form or call the 24-hour hotline. We aim to attend within 48 hours.
