FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

How do I track a parcel?

Enter your tracking number on the Track page or in our app.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

Yes, with remittance within two working days.

What is the weight limit?

Couriers carry up to 30 kg. Freight handles pallets and full loads.

Can I integrate with my shop?

Plug-ins for Shopee, Lazada, Shopify and WooCommerce plus an open API.

What if a parcel is damaged?

Every parcel is insured up to RM 500. Higher cover is available.

Do you deliver to Sabah and Sarawak?

Yes, by air and sea with delivery in 3–5 days.
