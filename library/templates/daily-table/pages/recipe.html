Sheet-pan chicken & vegetables

Everything on one tray, 35 minutes, and the leftovers make tomorrow's lunch.

10prep min
25cook min
4servings
$$cost
  1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Cut potatoes and carrots into 2 cm pieces.
  2. Toss with oil, salt and garlic on a large tray. Roast 10 minutes.
  3. Add chicken thighs, skin up, and roast 25 minutes until browned and 75°C inside.
  4. Rest 5 minutes and spoon the pan juices over everything.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs
  • 600 g potatoes
  • 4 carrots
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves
