How to remove a red wine stain

Works on cotton, linen and most synthetics. Faster is better.

  1. Blot the stain with a dry cloth. Press, don't rub — rubbing spreads it.
  2. Cover it with a thick layer of salt. Leave 10 minutes while it pulls the wine up.
  3. Rinse from the back with cold water. Warm water sets the stain.
  4. Rub in a little dish soap and white vinegar, rest 5 minutes, rinse again.
  5. Wash as normal. Check it's gone before the dryer.

You'll need

  • Dry cloth or paper towel
  • Table salt
  • Dish soap and white vinegar

Don't

  • Use hot water first
  • Use bleach on wool or silk
  • Dry it until the stain is gone
