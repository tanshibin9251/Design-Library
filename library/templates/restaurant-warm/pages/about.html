Three generations, one wok

Opened by Ah Moi in 1989 with four tables and a charcoal stove.

1989

Ah Moi opens the kopitiam in a shop-lot with a charcoal stove and a hand-written menu.

2008

Her son takes over the kitchen, adds the rendang and starts roasting coffee upstairs.

Today

Her granddaughter runs the floor. The recipes are the same; the stove is gas.
