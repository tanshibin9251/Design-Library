FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Do you take reservations?

Yes for groups of six or more. Otherwise walk in — we usually find you a table.

Is your food halal?

Yes, fully halal-certified.

Do you deliver?

Within 10 km, and through all major apps.

Can you cater for allergies?

Tell us in advance. We will separate preparation.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes — about a third of the menu.

Can I buy your sambal?

Yes, in jars at the counter.
