FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

What should I expect on Sunday?

Around 75 minutes of music, a short reflection, and tea and kuih afterwards. Come as you are.

Is there something for children?

Yes. Children's activities run alongside the gathering, with trained volunteers.

Do I need to be a member?

No. Everyone is welcome, whatever you believe or do not believe yet.

Is the hall accessible?

Yes, there is a ramp, a lift and an accessible toilet. Tell us if you need anything else.

How is giving used?

About 70 percent supports community work. Accounts are shared with the congregation each year.

Can I hire the hall?

The hall is available for community events on weekdays. Contact the office.
