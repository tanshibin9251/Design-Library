Who we are

A small community in Petaling Jaya that eats together before it does anything else.

We began in 2011 as eight people around a kitchen table. Today about a hundred and twenty of us meet each Sunday, from toddlers to grandparents, in a converted shop-lot with a very good kitchen.

We are not here to fix anyone. We're here to listen, cook, pray and help one another get through the week.

  • Everyone is welcome.Whatever you believe or don't, whoever you love, however long it has been.
  • Questions are allowed.Our best conversations start with "I'm not sure about this."
  • Food comes first.We eat together, then we talk about anything else.
