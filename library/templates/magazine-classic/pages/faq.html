FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Is it free to read?

Most stories are free. Members get unlimited access to our archive and long reads.

How do I cancel?

One click in your account. You keep access until the period ends.

Can I republish an article?

Contact our syndication desk with the story link and intended use.

How do I send a tip?

Email tips@themeridian.example — we protect sources.

Do you accept corrections?

Always. Use the corrections link at the end of any article.

Do you take advertising?

Yes, clearly labelled and separate from editorial.
