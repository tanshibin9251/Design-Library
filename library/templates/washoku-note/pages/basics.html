基本の基本

Two things to get right before anything else.

出汁

Dashi in 20 minutes

  1. Soak 10 g kombu in 1 litre water for 15 minutes.

  2. Heat gently; remove kombu just before boiling.

  3. Add 20 g katsuobushi, turn off, wait 2 minutes and strain.

ご飯

Rice, the Japanese way

  1. Rinse until the water is nearly clear.

  2. Soak 30 minutes. Use 1:1.1 rice to water.

  3. Cook, then rest covered for 10 minutes.
