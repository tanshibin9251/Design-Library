照り焼きチキン

Teriyaki chicken

材料 · Serves 2

  • Chicken thigh2 pieces
  • Soy sauce2 tbsp
  • Mirin2 tbsp
  • Sake1 tbsp
  • Sugar1 tsp
  • Oil1 tsp

  1. Pat the thighs dry and prick the skin with a fork. This helps the sauce cling.

  2. Pan-fry skin-side down over medium heat for 6 minutes without moving, until deep golden.

  3. Flip, cover and cook 4 minutes. Wipe out the excess fat with kitchen paper.

  4. Add the sauce and let it reduce, spooning it over until glossy. Slice and serve over rice.
