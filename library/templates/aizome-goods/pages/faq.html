よくある質問

Will indigo stain my skin or clothes?

Slightly, in the first washes. Wash alone and the transfer fades.

Can I request a specific shade?

Yes. Choose one of the six shades or send us a swatch. Allow 10 working days.

Do you do wholesale?

Yes, minimum 20 pieces. Use the contact form.

Is it vegan?

Yes. Indigo is plant-based; our vat uses wood ash and wheat bran.
