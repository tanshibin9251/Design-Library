Indigo is a living colour. A few habits keep it blue for years.

01

Wash alone

For the first three washes, in cold water, with a neutral soap.

02

No bleach

Not even oxygen bleach. It strips the blue.

03

Dry in shade

Direct sun fades indigo unevenly.

04

Re-dye

Send it back. We will bring the colour up again.
