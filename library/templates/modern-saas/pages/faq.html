FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free forever for individuals with up to three projects.

Can I change plans later?

Any time. Upgrades are instant and downgrades apply at the next billing date.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Verified non-profits and students get 50% off any paid plan.

How secure is my data?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with SOC 2 Type II audited controls.

Can I import from other tools?

Yes — one-click import from Trello, Asana, Jira and CSV.

Do you have an API?

A full REST API and webhooks are available on every plan.
