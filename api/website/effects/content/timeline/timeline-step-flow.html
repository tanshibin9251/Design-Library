How a project unfolds

Four clear stages, with a review at the end of each one.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We listen, audit and agree on what success looks like.

    1 week
  2. 02

    Define

    Sitemap, content outline and a visual direction to react to.

    2 weeks
  3. 03

    Design

    Key screens, type and motion, refined together with you.

    3 weeks
  4. 04

    Deliver

    Build, test, launch, and a calm handover session.

    2 weeks
